REPRODUCE / UPDATE
Raw inputs are ignored under data/geology/; bundled derived assets are under
 dashboard/geology/. Import script: scripts/import_geology.py.
Download the IPIS CSV as data/geology/ipis.csv. Download the USGS catalogue
JSON, follow its Africa_GIS.gdb.zip downloadUri, save as data/geology/africa.zip,
and unpack it to data/geology/Africa_GIS.gdb. Use a Python venv with fiona.
Run python scripts/import_geology.py, then rebuild the Rust binary. The output
metadata contains input SHA-256 hashes and import time. Changing upstream
schemas or unexpected DRC coordinates causes import failure, not silent repair.
The served dataset is a pinned snapshot, not a six-hour mining-data collector.

USAGE
Situation room > Minerals & geology: independent IPIS / USGS toggles,
source-native mineral filter, date/source popups, filtered GeoJSON export.
GET /v1/geology/sites returns the complete attributed FeatureCollection.
GET /geology/congo-1931.jpg returns the archived scan.
Source mineral labels are preserved in French/English to avoid lossy mapping;
UI instructions are bilingual. No mineral abundance or economic value is
inferred. Coltan, cobalt and copper are not automatically called rare earths.

INTERACTIVE EXPLORATION UPDATE
Map points now cluster below zoom 12; cluster clicks expand the group. Counts
are records, not necessarily distinct physical mines. Search matches names,
province and source mineral labels with case/accent normalization. Province,
record-type, commodity and source filters combine. Zoom to results fits their
extent. The directory supports keyboard selection and incremental browsing.
Selecting a site highlights it and opens an inspector with exports and nearby
news context at 10/25/50/100 km. Distances use the haversine formula against
approximate place mentions in currently loaded, filtered news. Each article
appears once at its nearest mention. Null coordinates are excluded. Proximity
is not evidence that a report concerns the mine. Map expansion, compass and
metric scale assist exploration. No new paid data service was added.
Validation: node tests/map_interactions.cjs (filters, distance, missing data,
clustering lifecycle, selection clearing and style restoration).

4. Macrostrat geology tiles and point lookup — CC BY 4.0.
   Tiles: https://tiles.macrostrat.org/carto/{z}/{x}/{y}.png (CORS-enabled).
   Point query: https://macrostrat.org/api/v2/geologic_units/map?lat=&lng=
   For the DRC the underlying map is mainly CGMW-BRGM (2016), New edition of
   the 1:10,000,000 geological map of Africa (Thiéblemont, ed.). Small-scale:
   unit boundaries are generalised; not suitable for site-level geology.
   GET /v1/geology/unit?lat=&lng= proxies one lookup, cached per ~100 m cell,
   returning lithology, age interval, description and the source map citation.

5. World Bank Commodity Price Data ("Pink Sheet"), monthly — CC BY 4.0.
   https://www.worldbank.org/en/research/commodity-markets (CMO-Historical-
   Data-Monthly.xlsx, sheet "Monthly Prices"). Series used: Copper, Tin,
   Zinc, Gold, Silver, Platinum, Crude oil Brent. Synced daily. These are
   global benchmark prices for refined metal, NOT prices paid at DRC sites.
   No public benchmark exists here for cobalt, coltan, tungsten or diamonds.

6. UN Comtrade public preview API — UN Comtrade terms; attribution required.
   https://comtradeapi.un.org/public/v1/preview/C/A/HS (one period per call).
   Weekly: for the last three years and the HS codes in config/commodities.json
   (2603/7402/7403 copper; 2605/2822/8105 cobalt; 2609/8001 tin; 2615 coltan;
   2611 tungsten; 7108 gold; 7102 diamonds; 2608 zinc; 7106 silver; 7110
   platinum; 2602 manganese; 282520/283691 lithium; 2709 crude oil):
   - partner-reported imports whose partner is the DRC ("who buys");
   - DRC-reported exports by destination.
   Unit values (USD/kg) are customs value ÷ net weight and stand in for a
   price where no benchmark exists. Buyers are FIRST RECORDED IMPORTING
   COUNTRIES (transit hubs included, e.g. Namibia/Zambia for cobalt
   hydroxide), not companies or end users. Partner and DRC declarations often
   disagree (codes, transit, under-reporting); the UI shows both and flags
   large gaps. 490 "Other Asia, nes" is Comtrade's code for Taiwan and is kept.

Site dossier additions (scripts/import_geology.py):
- IPIS (latest visit): workers, mechanisation, mercury use, state services,
  traceability scheme, Ministry of Mines qualification (green/yellow/red/blue),
  and per mineral its selling points and final destination. Armed-actor
  fields remain excluded, as before.
- USGS facilities (end-2018): facility type, operator, owners with stakes,
  operating status and note, nameplate capacity and unit.
- "Indicative capacity value" = nameplate capacity × latest benchmark price,
  only for mass/volume units with a benchmark. It is a ceiling for
  orientation, not production, revenue, reserves or current output.

MINERAL FOCUS (added 18 October 2026)
Click a mineral (market ticker, market chip, a mineral tag in a site dossier,
or "Map … sites & mining areas"), or open /#focus/<commodity id or label>.
- Sites of that mineral (respecting the IPIS/USGS toggles) are grouped into
  mining areas: by territory (IPIS) or province (USGS, no territory field),
  then single-linkage within 20 km; areas need at least 3 sites. Grouping by
  administrative unit first stops dense belts merging into region-sized blobs.
- Each area is outlined as a buffered convex hull (7 km margin) in the
  mineral's colour, numbered and labelled "territory · province · sites".
  Everything outside the areas is dimmed (overlapping outlines merge into one
  spotlight hole). Only that mineral's sites remain on the map.
- Navigation is animated: overview of all areas, then a tilted fly-through
  of the six largest, then back to the overview. Any drag, scroll or touch
  stops the tour; "Replay tour", "All areas" and each area row fly on demand.
  With reduced motion enabled, moves are instant and no tour plays.
- Areas describe where historical site records cluster. They are NOT
  concession boundaries, ore bodies, reserves or zones of armed control.
Validation: node tests/mineral_focus.cjs (linkage, minimum size, territory
separation, outlines contain sites, mask winding and non-overlapping holes,
real gold data).

HISTORICAL PROVINCE STATISTICS (19 October 2026)
scripts/import_historical_ipis.py derives dashboard/geology/historical-ipis.json
from the pinned raw CSV above. Only 2009–2023 visits in Nord-Kivu, Sud-Kivu,
and Ituri are included. Select the latest visit per pcode per province/year,
using vid to break date ties. This is distinct from the latest-ever mineral
inventory: no forward filling between years. 37 province/year rows, 4,259
site/year observations. The same site can occur in different years.
Presence and interference retain yes/no/unknown counts. Actor names are
counted once per site/year across three actor slots; actor mention does not
attribute the site's interference flag to that actor. Names remain verbatim.
The export includes no mine coordinates, town names, or military boundaries.
Source hash, attribution ODC-BY 1.0, and reproducible import retained. These
are later-download historical observations, not the original 2023 publication
sample or a reconstruction of knowledge available at each historical date.
UI: Historical situation > IPIS mining observations. This is a pinned dataset,
not a scheduled remote collector. The separate news-candidate worker remains
hourly. UN PDF archive is linked as a reference, not imported control claims.
Validation: python3 scripts/test_historical_ipis.py.
